## Amendment 2026-11-07 (written before the free-dev-c8 result): deterministic director

The owner asked for a deterministic "director" (rules over the raw ticket) to raise accuracy. Prototype: `evals/director/director.py` (two one-way rules: a bare reference cannot be `specific`; the user's own refund or compensation demand is `risky`), replayed by `evals/director/replay.py` over recorded Groq trials. The director only post-processes the features, so the replay is the exact score of the full system on those trials (the replay without the director reproduces the recorded 157/192 of c6).

- c6 on Groq: 157/192 recorded, 182/192 with the director (answer 122/126, escalate 27/30, request_info 33/36, no trial made worse). In-sample: the rules were written after reading the development tickets.
- New decision rule, replacing the one above: after `free-dev-c8` completes, compute `python evals/director/replay.py free-dev-c8`. The candidate is `f2b + director` only if its replayed score is at least 187/192 (5 above c6 + director = 182); otherwise `c6 + director`.
- The director is integrated into the runner only after `free-dev-c8` has completed (the campaign fingerprint covers `scripts/`). Its sha256 is bound in the final authorization together with the prompt, schema, dataset and rubric.
- The final 20 cases must not be used to tune any rule; the director is frozen before the final authorization is written.
